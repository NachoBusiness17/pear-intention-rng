How This Experiment Works & Why Dead-Salmon Controls Matter

1. 200-Bit Trial Math

Each trial sums M = 200 random bits (25 bytes). Expected sum is 100 with standard deviation √50 ≈ 7.07. Running cumulative deviation is D_n = Σ(X_k - 100) and Stouffer score is Z_n = D_n / √(50n).

2. Why ±1.96σ & Pre-Registration

A random walk naturally wanders as √n. Watching live and stopping at a peak inflated 50-trial false positives from 4% (fixed N=50) to 33% in a 100-run Dead-Salmon test, while raw Z != 0 leans fired on 99/100 noise runs.

3. Per-Byte Provenance

When hosted on Vercel, /api/qrng queries ANU QRNG and falls back to secrets on outages. Opened directly offline or on GitHub Pages, it uses crypto.getRandomValues. Every trial hashes its exact raw bytes with SHA-256.
